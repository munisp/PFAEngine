PFAEngine Member is the self-service app for your Retirement Savings Account (RSA). Check your pension anytime — even on a slow connection.

BALANCE & CONTRIBUTIONS
• See your RSA balance and how it has grown, month by month.
• Track employer and employee contributions as they land.
• Make voluntary contributions (AVC/MPVC) and micro-pension top-ups.

STATEMENTS YOU CAN TRUST
• Generate and download RSA statements on demand.
• Every statement carries a QR code so employers, banks and mortgage providers can verify it instantly.

I-AM-ALIVE, WITHOUT THE QUEUE
• Pensioners: complete periodic "I am alive" verification from home with a short selfie check — no branch visit, no paper forms.
• Raw biometric images never leave the secure verification flow; only the liveness result is recorded.

RETIREMENT & CLAIMS
• Book retirement counselling sessions.
• Start and track benefit, NSITF and death-benefit claims with document upload and status tracking.

YOUR FAMILY, COVERED
• Keep next-of-kin and beneficiary details up to date so your savings reach the right people.

KNOW YOUR FUND
• Compare fund performance (Fund I–IV, retiree funds) and see unit prices over time.
• Plain-language guides on how the Contributory Pension Scheme works — in English, with more Nigerian languages on the way.

BUILT FOR NIGERIAN NETWORKS
• Works on low-end Android phones and unstable data: the app keeps your last-known balance and statements available offline and syncs when you're back online.
• Light on data, light on storage.

SECURE & REGULATED
• Your account is operated by a PenCom-licensed Pension Fund Administrator.
• Login with your RSA PIN; sensitive actions require step-up verification.
• Data is encrypted in transit and handled in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA). We never sell your data.

QUESTIONS?
In-app support connects you to your PFA's member-care team.

Note: PFAEngine is the technology platform; your retirement savings are held and administered by your licensed PFA under the regulation of the National Pension Commission (PenCom).

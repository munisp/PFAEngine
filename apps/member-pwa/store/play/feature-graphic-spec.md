# Feature graphic spec (Google Play)

- **Size/format**: 1024 × 500 px, PNG or 24-bit JPEG, no alpha.
- **Safe zone**: keep all text/logo within the central 1024×440; Play crops
  and overlays UI at the edges.
- **Content**:
  - Left 60%: brand mark + wordmark "PFAEngine Member" (or the tenant PFA's
    brand — this is a white-label platform; produce one feature graphic per
    deploying PFA).
  - Right 40%: phone mockup showing the Dashboard screen (balance + sparkline).
  - Tagline (max one line): "Your pension, in your pocket."
- **Palette**: app background `#f7f4ee` family; brand accent from
  `public/theme.json` of the deploying tenant.
- **Do not**: include store badges, device frames with competitor branding,
  photos of real people without releases, or return-rate promises
  (financial-promotion rules — avoid "guaranteed growth" language).
- **File name**: `feature-graphic-1024x500.png`, stored with brand assets
  (not in git).
